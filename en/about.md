Welcome to **Dev Notebook**, where someone who doesn't write code builds things by talking them through with AI.

Right now the main project is a fantasy web game called **Adventure Notebook: DO NOT READ!!** (Notebook RPG for short). Instead of playing a chosen hero, you're born as an ordinary villager, live out a whole life rolling a 20-sided die, and finish with a gravestone and a cheerful **"Great job!"** stamp on the final page.

Here I collect the weekly build logs, the balance mysteries we solved with numbers, and the practical habits I've picked up working with AI as an entire engineering team.

## How to read this notebook

- Posts fall into four main topics (**Notebook RPG devlog**, **Working with AI**, **Dev tips**, and **AI lab**). Pick whichever catches your eye under **Topics** in the top menu.
- Ongoing stories are grouped under **Series**—a great place to binge the weekly dev logs from episode one.
- Click any tag on a post to see other entries wrestling with the same problem.
- Looking for something specific? Hit **Search** at the top right (or press `/`) to jump straight there.
- Screenshots inside posts may still show Korean UI text.
